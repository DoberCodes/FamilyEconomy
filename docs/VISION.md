# Vision

## Purpose

Family Economy is a family-focused financial literacy platform designed to teach children responsible money management through a fictional household economy.

The platform exists to teach earning, saving, budgeting, goal setting, delayed gratification, taxes, community responsibility, and financial decision making. Family Economy is an educational platform first and a software product second.

## Target Audience

- Parents who want a lightweight family finance system for kids.
- Children aged 6-14 who need an age-appropriate way to earn, save, budget, and spend fictional educational credits.
- Families who want shared visibility into jobs, savings goals, reward requests, community funds, and family pool items.

## Core Principles

### Educational First

Prioritize educational value over entertainment value. Features should help children understand financial concepts and build healthy financial habits.

### Fictional Currency

All credits, balances, goals, rewards, statements, and transactions represent fictional educational credits. The platform is not a bank, payment processor, payroll system, or real-money account.

### Parent Authority

Parents are the governing authority. Parents control rules, tax policies, rewards, jobs, allowances, community spending decisions, and approval settings. Children participate within the framework established by parents.

### Goals Are Core

Savings goals are a primary educational mechanism. Children should be encouraged to save toward meaningful goals, track progress, make spending decisions, and experience delayed gratification.

### Family Tax Philosophy

Family taxes, when implemented, should teach shared responsibility, community participation, and how collective resources benefit a group. Taxes should not be framed as punishment.

### Community Matters

The product should reinforce that financial decisions affect both individuals and groups through family funds, shared projects, family-wide benefits, and collaborative decision making.

### Avoid Excessive Gamification

The experience can be warm and motivating, but should avoid loot boxes, gambling systems, randomized rewards, manipulative engagement loops, excessive streak pressure, artificial scarcity, and engagement-first design patterns.

## Product Direction

Family Economy aims to become the go-to mobile-first family economy dashboard where:

- Kids can request rewards, save toward goals, and claim jobs without feeling overwhelmed.
- Parents can review and approve jobs, reward ideas, savings goals, tax settings, and family fund decisions with clear options and family-level policies.
- The family can balance personal rewards with shared pool items, encourage goal-based saving, and practice community-minded financial decision making.

We are building toward a system that feels like a friendly learning dashboard rather than accounting software, with parent-led guardrails and child-focused momentum.
