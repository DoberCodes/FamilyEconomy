## Core Philosophy

Family Economy is a customizable educational household economy, not just a chore app.

The system is designed to teach:

1. Responsibility
2. Saving
3. Spending
4. Delayed gratification
5. Resource management
6. Community contribution
7. Financial literacy
8. Family collaboration

Goal: introduce real-world economic concepts in age-appropriate ways.

## Foundation Snapshot

## Phase Intent

### Phase 2 - Responsibility and Recognition

Purpose: separate responsibility from compensation.

Scope:

1. XP Responsibility System (for example: clean room, homework, help sibling, follow house rules)
2. Achievement System (for example: first goal, consistent contributor, family helper, reading champion)
3. Family Recognition (for example: most helpful, longest streak, goal setter)

### Phase 3 - Community and Family Contribution

Purpose: teach that economies are larger than individuals.

Scope:

1. Optional family tax rates (for example: 5%, 10%, 15%)
2. Family Fund visible to everyone
3. Family Fund usage flows (for example: pizza night, family movie night, zoo trip)
4. Family voting for rewards, goals, and fund usage

### Phase 4 - Economic Concepts

Purpose: introduce simple economic systems.

Scope:

1. Scarcity expansion: limited reward/job availability
2. Supply and demand behavior tuning
3. Seasonal events (for example: summer bonus week, double earnings weekend, holiday events)
4. Community projects (for example: garden project, camping trip, board game fund)

### Phase 5 - Advanced Family Economy

Purpose: introduce more realistic financial concepts.

Scope:

1. Parent matching for savings goals
2. Family grants (education, reading, family project goals)
3. Optional allowance integration (convert credits or keep educational-only currency)

### Phase 6 - Teen Expansion

Purpose: prepare older children for adulthood.

Scope:

1. Budgeting and savings categories
2. Emergency savings concepts
3. Investing simulation (educational only, no real money)
4. Small business simulation (for example: lawn care, lemonade stand, pet sitting)

### Phase 7 - Family Governance

Purpose: teach decision-making and collaboration.

Scope:

1. Family voting for goals/activities/fund usage
2. Proposal system (reward ideas, family goals, new jobs)
3. Expanded negotiation and compromise workflows

### Phase 8 - Ecosystem Expansion

Gate: only after product-market fit.

Scope:

1. Raspberry Pi Family Hub dashboard
2. Classroom economy variant
3. Homeschool economy variant
